# Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the CTA Helper app (hereby referred to as
"Application") for mobile devices that was created by Timothy Morgan (hereby
referred to as "Service Provider") as an Open Source service. This service is
intended for use "AS IS".

## What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?

The Application collects limited data to provide its core functionality and
improve reliability. The specific categories of data are described below.

### Location Data

The Application uses your device's location, with your permission, solely to
list the airports nearest you. Location data is resolved entirely on your device
and is never transmitted to the Service Provider, to any server, or to any third
party. Granting location access is optional: every other part of the Application
works without it, and airports can always be found by identifier or city name
instead.

### Crash and Diagnostic Data

The Application uses Sentry, a third-party error monitoring service, to collect
crash reports, performance data, and diagnostic information. This data helps the
Service Provider identify and fix bugs. Sentry may collect device information,
app state at the time of a crash, and performance traces. This data is not
linked to your identity and is used solely for app stability and performance
improvement.

### Network Requests

The Application downloads two kinds of public data: navigation data cycles,
published as GitHub releases, and aviation weather observations (METARs),
published by aviationweather.gov. These requests carry no account, identifier,
or personal information. Requesting a weather observation necessarily discloses
the airport identifier being requested to that service.

### Tracking

The Application does not track you. It does not use advertising identifiers,
does not engage in cross-app tracking, and does not share data with advertisers
or data brokers. The Application has no user accounts and collects no personally
identifiable information.

## Do third parties see and/or have access to information obtained by the Application?

Crash and diagnostic data is transmitted to Sentry (a third-party service) for
error monitoring. Weather and navigation data requests are made to
aviationweather.gov and GitHub respectively, as described above. No other data
is shared with third parties. Location data is never sent to the Service
Provider or any third party.

## What are my opt-out rights?

You can revoke location access at any time in the device's Settings app, without
affecting any other part of the Application. You can stop all collection of
information by the Application easily by uninstalling it. You may use the
standard uninstall processes as may be available as part of your mobile device
or via the mobile application marketplace or network.

## Children

The Application is not used to knowingly solicit data from or market to children
under the age of 13.

The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personally identifiable
information from children. The Service Provider encourages all children to never
submit any personally identifiable information through the Application and/or
Services. The Service Provider encourages parents and legal guardians to monitor
their children's Internet usage and to help enforce this Policy by instructing
their children never to provide personally identifiable information through the
Application and/or Services without their permission. If you have reason to
believe that a child has provided personally identifiable information to the
Service Provider through the Application and/or Services, please contact the
Service Provider (<apple@timothymorgan.info>) so that they will be able to take
the necessary actions. You must also be at least 16 years of age to consent to
the processing of your personally identifiable information in your country (in
some countries we may allow your parent or guardian to do so on your behalf).

## Security

The Service Provider is concerned about safeguarding the confidentiality of your
information. Location data never leaves your device. Crash and diagnostic data
transmitted to Sentry is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), as are all
navigation and weather data requests.
